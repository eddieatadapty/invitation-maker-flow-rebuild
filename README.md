# Invitation Maker Growth Audit

**Live → https://eddieatadapty.github.io/invitation-maker-flow-rebuild/**

Five ideas to turn more Invitation Maker users into subscribers, each built into a clickable version of Invitation Maker's own onboarding. Prepared by Adapty.

| # | Idea | Potential outcome |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| 01 | Open on the designs they searched for, not on the paywall | +10–20% CR to purchase |
| 02 | Ask for Premium when they send, not before they start | +5–15% CR to purchase |
| 03 | Show the RSVP list before the price | +5–10% CR to purchase |
| 04 | Lead with your App Store proof, not Trustpilot’s | +5–10% CR to purchase |
| 05 | Offer one week to people with one event | +5–10% CR from paywall closers |

Every price, rating and feature comes from Invitation Maker's App Store listing, website or in-app screens. Impact ranges are Adapty’s expected effect from teardowns and A/B tests across 30,000+ subscription apps, not measured lift for Invitation Maker. They overlap, so they don’t add up.

Static single page, no build step: `python3 -m http.server`.
