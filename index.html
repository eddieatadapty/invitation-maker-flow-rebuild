Day 1

Pass the Apple Ads keyword into Adapty

After a one-time SDK setup, the keyword each install came from arrives as a user attribute and picks the flow at first open. No app release.

Week 1

One flow per kind of search

Graduation and birthday open on their own designs; “invitation”, “greeting” and your own name open on the question “what are you celebrating?”.

Week 2

Judge each search on what it earns

Each search’s flow against today’s, on trials, paid subscriptions and what each install from that search earns, so bids follow the searches that pay.

Your Apple Ads, in one paragraph. You show on about 44% and 39% of US searches for your own app names (“invitation maker, cards & rsvp”, “invitation maker: cards & rsvp”), so more than half the people looking for you meet someone else’s ad first. On “birthday”, the most popular term on your list, you show on about 1%; on “graduation”, 32%. Some terms bring people who aren’t making an invitation: “invitae” (a genetic-testing company, 15%), “nautik for kubernetes”, “disney checklist”. That data is account-level, so it can’t tell Invitation Maker from Wishwave. We found no active Greetings Island ads in the Meta Ad Library on 11 November 2026. This layer carries no range: only a test per search can give one.
